## The Opportunity

Joined Growth Startup at a pivotal moment—product-market fit was emerging, but scaling was the challenge. In 18 months, we grew from 50K to 150K active users.

## My Focus Areas

### 1. Growth Engine

Built the systems and processes for sustainable growth:

**Acquisition**:
- Optimized onboarding flow (2x completion rate)
- Launched referral program (drove 30% of new users)
- Improved SEO and content strategy

**Activation**:
- Defined "activation" metric through data analysis
- Built personalized onboarding paths
- Reduced time-to-value by 60%

**Retention**:
- Implemented engagement loops
- Created re-engagement campaigns
- Improved D30 retention by 40%

### 2. Experimentation Culture

Transformed how we made decisions:

**Before**:
- Decisions based on HiPPO (highest paid person's opinion)
- Long development cycles
- Infrequent releases

**After**:
- Every feature starts as an experiment
- Weekly experiment reviews
- 5x more experiments per quarter

**The Framework**:
```
1. Hypothesis: If we [change], then [metric] will [improve/decrease] by [amount]
2. Design: Minimal viable test
3. Execute: Run for statistical significance
4. Learn: Document findings, decide next steps
```

### 3. Team Building

Grew from IC to managing 3 PMs:

- Hired 2 PMs (100+ candidates screened)
- Created PM onboarding program
- Established career ladder and growth framework

## Key Projects

### Project: Onboarding 2.0

**Problem**: 60% of signups never completed onboarding.

**Solution**: 
- Shortened flow from 8 steps to 3
- Added progress indicators
- Personalized based on use case

**Results**:
- Onboarding completion: 60% → 85%
- Time to first value: 10 min → 4 min
- D7 retention: +25%

### Project: Referral Program

**Problem**: High CAC, low organic growth.

**Solution**:
- Two-sided incentive (giver and receiver)
- Easy sharing mechanics
- Tracking and attribution

**Results**:
- 30% of new users from referrals
- CAC reduced by 40%
- Viral coefficient: 0.3

## Lessons from Hypergrowth

### What Worked
1. **Focus**: Said "no" to 80% of ideas to focus on what mattered
2. **Speed**: Shipped weekly, learned constantly
3. **Metrics**: Clear dashboards everyone could access

### What Was Hard
1. **Technical debt**: Fast shipping created cleanup work later
2. **Team scaling**: Processes that worked at 5 broke at 15
3. **Burnout**: Hypergrowth is exhausting; sustainability matters

## Why I Left

After 18 months, Growth Startup was in great shape:
- Clear path to profitability
- Strong team in place
- Product flywheel spinning

I was ready for a new challenge—leading a larger team and tackling new problems. The timing was right for both me and the company.
