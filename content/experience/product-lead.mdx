## Role Overview

As Product Lead at Tech Company, I'm responsible for the strategy and execution of our core platform, which serves millions of users globally.

## Key Responsibilities

### Product Strategy
- Define product vision and roadmap
- Prioritize initiatives based on impact and effort
- Align stakeholders on direction

### Team Leadership
- Manage team of 8 (3 PMs, 3 designers, 2 researchers)
- Run weekly planning and retrospectives
- Mentor junior team members

### Execution
- Drive cross-functional initiatives from concept to launch
- Partner closely with engineering on technical decisions
- Monitor metrics and iterate based on data

## Major Initiatives

### 1. Engagement Redesign

**Challenge**: User engagement had plateaued despite growing user base.

**Approach**:
- Conducted extensive user research (50+ interviews)
- Analyzed behavioral data to identify drop-off points
- Ran 20+ A/B tests to validate hypotheses

**Results**:
- 40% increase in weekly active users
- 25% improvement in retention
- Featured in company all-hands as exemplar project

### 2. Enterprise Expansion

**Challenge**: Needed to grow enterprise segment without compromising core product.

**Approach**:
- Interviewed 30+ enterprise prospects
- Identified key gaps (SSO, admin controls, compliance)
- Built dedicated enterprise team

**Results**:
- Launched enterprise tier in 6 months
- Closed 15 enterprise deals in first quarter
- $2M+ in new ARR

### 3. Platform Performance

**Challenge**: Page load times were hurting conversion.

**Approach**:
- Partnered with engineering on performance audit
- Prioritized highest-impact optimizations
- Created performance monitoring dashboard

**Results**:
- 60% reduction in page load time
- 15% improvement in conversion rate
- Zero performance regressions since launch

## What I've Learned

### On Leadership
> "Your job is to make your team successful, not to be the smartest person in the room."

### On Data
Data informs decisions; it doesn't make them. The best PMs know when to trust intuition.

### On Speed
> "Move fast and break things" is wrong. "Move fast and learn things" is better.
