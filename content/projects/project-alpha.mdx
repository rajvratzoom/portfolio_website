## Overview

Project Alpha is a comprehensive platform designed to revolutionize how teams collaborate on complex problems. Built from the ground up with a focus on user experience and scalability.

## The Problem

Teams across industries struggle with:

- **Fragmented communication** - Information scattered across multiple tools
- **Lack of context** - Team members missing crucial background information
- **Inefficient workflows** - Manual processes that slow down decision-making

## My Role

As the product lead, I was responsible for:

1. Defining the product vision and roadmap
2. Conducting extensive user research (50+ interviews)
3. Creating detailed user personas and journey maps
4. Working closely with engineering on technical feasibility
5. Leading sprint planning and prioritization

## Approach

### Discovery Phase

We started with a comprehensive discovery phase:

- Interviewed 50+ potential users across 10 companies
- Analyzed competitor products and identified gaps
- Created detailed user personas

### Design & Prototyping

Using insights from research, we:

- Developed low-fidelity wireframes
- Conducted usability testing with 20 participants
- Iterated based on feedback

### Development & Launch

- Worked in 2-week sprints
- Implemented feature flags for gradual rollout
- Launched beta to 100 users, then expanded

## Key Features

### Real-time Collaboration

Teams can work together simultaneously with live cursors and instant updates.

### Smart Search

AI-powered search that understands context and surfaces relevant information.

### Integration Hub

Connects with 50+ popular tools including Slack, Notion, and Jira.

## Results

- **40% increase** in team productivity (measured via task completion rates)
- **10,000+ users** in first 6 months
- **4.8/5 average rating** on G2 and Capterra
- Featured in **TechCrunch** and **Product Hunt** (#2 Product of the Day)

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, PostgreSQL
- **Infrastructure**: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes

## Lessons Learned

1. **Start with user research** - Our initial assumptions were often wrong
2. **Ship fast, iterate faster** - Early feedback was invaluable
3. **Invest in onboarding** - First impressions matter enormously
4. **Build for extensibility** - Our integration strategy drove adoption
