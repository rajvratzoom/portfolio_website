## The Story

Beta Ventures started as a side project—a Slack community for product researchers. It grew into a platform connecting 10,000+ researchers and practitioners, and was eventually acquired in 2023.

## Origin

In 2021, I noticed a gap:

- **Researchers** had valuable insights but limited reach
- **Practitioners** needed research but couldn't access it
- **Both** wanted to connect but had no venue

I started a Slack community with 50 people I knew. It grew organically from there.

## What We Built

### The Platform

- **Research Library**: Curated, practitioner-friendly summaries
- **Expert Directory**: Find and connect with specialists
- **Discussion Forums**: Moderated, high-signal conversations
- **Events**: Weekly AMAs, monthly workshops

### The Community

More than software, we built a genuine community:
- 68% monthly active rate
- 40+ volunteer moderators
- 200+ expert contributors
- NPS of 75

## Business Model

We kept it simple:
- **Free tier**: Access to forums and basic library
- **Pro tier** ($20/mo): Full library, expert access, events
- **Team tier** ($50/seat/mo): Team features, custom research

Revenue at acquisition: ~$500K ARR

## The Acquisition

In early 2023, we were approached by [Larger Platform], who saw strategic value in our community and content library. After 3 months of diligence, we closed the deal.

### Why I Sold

1. **Strategic fit**: They could scale the mission faster
2. **Team opportunity**: Great roles for everyone
3. **Personal readiness**: Ready for next chapter

## Lessons Learned

### On Community Building

> "Communities are gardens, not factories. You can't manufacture belonging."

Key principles:
1. **Quality over quantity** - 100 engaged members > 10,000 passive ones
2. **Moderation is curation** - Every removal decision shapes culture
3. **Give before you ask** - We gave away value for 18 months before charging

### On Solo Founding

I built Beta Ventures without co-founders. Pros and cons:

**Pros:**
- Fast decision-making
- Full ownership
- Flexibility

**Cons:**
- Lonely
- No complementary skills
- Harder to fundraise

For my next venture (Startup Alpha), I found co-founders.

### On Exits

The acquisition process taught me:
- Start building relationships with potential acquirers early
- Keep clean books and documentation
- Negotiate for your team, not just yourself
