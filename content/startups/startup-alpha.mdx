## The Vision

Startup Alpha is building the future of collaborative work. We believe that the best ideas emerge when diverse perspectives come together in the right environment.

## The Problem We're Solving

Modern teams face a paradox:
- More tools than ever to collaborate
- Yet collaboration feels harder than ever

Why? Because tools optimize for **individual productivity**, not **collective intelligence**.

## Our Approach

We're building a platform that:

1. **Surfaces the right people** for any given problem
2. **Creates the right context** for productive discussions
3. **Captures collective knowledge** for future reference

## What I've Learned

### On Fundraising

> "Fundraising is a means to an end, not an end itself. Raise enough to hit milestones, not so much that you lose urgency."

### On Hiring

The first 10 hires define your culture. We optimize for:
1. Skill (can they do the job?)
2. Will (do they want to do the job?)
3. Culture add (what unique perspective do they bring?)

### On Product

Ship early, ship often. Our first version was embarrassingly simple—but it got us our first 10 customers, who shaped everything that followed.
